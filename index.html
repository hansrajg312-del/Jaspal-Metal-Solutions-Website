About Us

A trusted name in
specialty scrap trading.

Jaspal Metal Solutions LLP is a professionally managed metal-scrap trading company based in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra, serving customers and suppliers across India. We specialize in the sourcing and trading of used tungsten carbide scrap, high-speed steel scrap and alloy steel scrap, with a focus on reliable sourcing, material identification, competitive commercial terms and dependable handling of industrial scrap.

Our Bhiwandi location provides access to a major industrial and logistics network in the Mumbai region, supporting efficient movement of material.

Every transaction is handled with a professional, B2B-first approach: clear communication, careful material verification and consistent commercial terms. Suppliers and buyers alike know exactly what to expect when they work with us.
